"Tears of Steel.mp4" is one minute of the film (from 0:19 to 1:19 of the
official 720p release, download.blender.org/demo/movies/ToS/), re-encoded
to H.264 and AAC. The subtitles in subs/ are the film's official
subtitles from download.blender.org/demo/movies/ToS/subtitles/, cut to the
same minute and saved as UTF-8; their text is unchanged.

Lexpad bundles it for "Try a one-minute clip" on Player home and credits
it there, while it plays, and in About Lexpad.
